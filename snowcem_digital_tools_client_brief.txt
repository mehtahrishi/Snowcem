EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
Snowcem Paints has built two next-generation interactive digital tools directly 
into the website platform. These tools streamline the customer decision-making 
process, eliminate paint quantity guesswork, and boost customer confidence 
before purchasing.


1. PAINT BUDGET CALCULATOR
--------------------------
Tagline: "From guesswork to precision budgeting in under 60 seconds."

What it Does:
An instant project estimation tool that allows homeowners, contractors, and 
architects to compute exact paint volume requirements and total project cost 
based on carpet area or surface dimensions.

Key Differentiators to Highlight with Clients:
• Pincode & Region-Aware Estimations: Unlike generic static online calculators, 
  our tool accounts for local coverage dynamics (interior vs. exterior, 
  masonry vs. concrete surfaces).
• Multi-Surface & Primer Inclusion: Automatically calculates required wall 
  primer, undercoats, and surface preparation materials alongside topcoats 
  for 100% accurate budgeting.
• Instant Quotation Export: Allows customers or dealers to immediately share 
  a breakdown with their paint contractor or Snowcem representative.
• Frictionless Sales Pipeline: Converts site visitors directly into warm leads 
  by offering an instant option to request a verified Snowcem Home Painting 
  Services quote based on their calculated budget.


2. INTERACTIVE 3D COLOUR VISUALISER
-----------------------------------
Tagline: "Virtual room transformation with smart luminance & texture preservation."

What it Does:
A dynamic visualizer that lets users test any paint shade from the master 
Snowcem palette on real wall surfaces in real time.

Key Differentiators & Upcoming Roadmap Features to Highlight:
• NEW/UPCOMING: Custom Customer Room Photo Upload: Customers can upload 
  actual photos of their own home, rooms, or exterior facades, allowing 
  them to preview Snowcem shades on their real walls rather than generic stock 
  sample templates.
• Smart Texture & Lighting Preservation (Luminance Sensing): Standard web 
  visualizers use simple flat color overlays that look unnatural. Snowcem’s 
  visualizer preserves original wall shadows, light gradients, and masonry 
  textures so the preview looks hyper-realistic.
• Tap-to-Paint Flood Fill & Precision Controls:
  - Smart Tap: Tap any wall to instantly flood-fill the selected color with 
    adjustable color tolerance.
  - Precision Brush & Eraser: Fine-tune corners, window frames, and decorative 
    moldings with adjustable brush sizes.
• Instant Shade Match & Palette Synchronization: Directly linked to Snowcem’s 
  master shade catalog, showing exact shade names, color codes, and 
  complementary accent recommendations.
• Before / After Comparison & One-Click Download: Users can toggle between 
  the original space and painted space, or download a high-res PDF/image summary 
  to share with family or clients.


3. SUMMARY PITCH FOR CLIENT MEETINGS
